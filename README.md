Leveraging Latents for Segmentation & Classification of Breast Cancer Screening IR Thermograms (Master Thesis). Got ₹2,00,000 Research Fellowship under IndiaAI Mission by Government of India. Objective: AI-based breast-region segmentation, latent feature learning & Normal/Sick classification using IR thermograms. Dataset: Used DMR-IR thermography subset with 362 images -162 Normal, 100 Sick and 100 Unknown/OOD images. Approach: Developed DL based Advanced Sequential Breast Network (ASBNet) using ResNet50, combining bilateral segmentation, latent-space asymmetry features, multi-task learning & OOD detection for unknown/non-thermogram inputs. Results: Achieved 92.86% Validation Accuracy, 97.62% AUC, 90% Sensitivity, 95.24% Specificity & 0.913 F1-score. Publication: IEEE conference paper on 'Infrared Breast Thermography for Cancer Screening: A Review of Artificial Intelligence Architectures and Latent Feature Learning' at ICSCCC-2026
